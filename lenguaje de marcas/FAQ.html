SEAT FACTORIE FAQ

Your questions answered about our automotive production and services

What types of cars do you manufacture?

We specialize in manufacturing a wide range of vehicles including electric cars, SUVs, sedans, and hybrid models designed for both urban and off-road use.

Where is your factory located?

Our main production facility is located in Detroit, Michigan, with additional plants in Europe and Asia to meet global demand efficiently.

Do you offer factory tours?

Yes, we offer guided tours of our manufacturing plants. Visitors can observe the production line, learn about our assembly processes, and see our quality control in action. Booking in advance is required.

What safety standards do you follow?

We strictly adhere to international safety and quality standards, including ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certifications. Our facilities prioritize employee safety and sustainable manufacturing practices.

How can I become a supplier or partner?

We welcome partnerships with certified suppliers. Please contact our procurement department through the official website to submit your application and meet our qualification criteria.
